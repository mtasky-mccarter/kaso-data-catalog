============================================================
1. BUSINESS CONFIRMATION
============================================================

At purchase-order line level we use three UI fields related to the
supplier CoA certificate:

1. CoA certifikát
2. Šarža na CoA
3. Dátum expirácie na CoA

These values are used to record data from the supplier CoA certificate
received for the purchase order / delivery.

The same user-entry capability is not available on the receipt form.

The field "CoA certifikát" is a selection/status field.

Observed business values:
- empty
- Nie
- Áno

The values are selected from a lookup / code list.

OBJD_O.XML_DATA
